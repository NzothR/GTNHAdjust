ported to com.gtnhadjust.machine.reshaping and assets/gtnhadjust. Both the complete
structure and the optional EV structure are preserved. Material names, hatches,
parallel processing and lifecycle were adapted to GTNH 2.9.0. The godforge free
upgrade behavior was implemented against GTNH 2.9.0's ForgeOfGodsData interface.
